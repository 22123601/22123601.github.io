End of Life

Wiping Hardware

It's important to delete any personal information before you sell or dispose of your computer, so that it cannot be accessed by anybody else either by mistake or for malicious purposes. Also, if you have a device that is faulty, think about what personal data is stored on it before you dispose of it.

Taking Apart

Taking a computer apart before disposal is really important for protecting personal data and ensuring proper environmental disposal. It allows for the secure removal and destruction of storage devices to prevent data theft, and the separation of valuable and hazardous materials for safe and responsible recycling.

Data security

  • Remove storage drives:
    This is the most critical step for data security. Hard drives and solid-state drives (SSDs) contain sensitive information like passwords, financial details, and personal documents.

  • Prevent toxic leaks:
    • Computers contain hazardous materials such as lead, mercury, arsenic, and cadmium. If not properly recycled, these can leach into the soil and water from landfills, harming ecosystems and human health.

  • Consider data wiping first:
    Before physically destroying the drives, you can also use software to wipe the information, though physical destruction offers the highest level of security.

  • Reuse components:
    Functional components can be salvaged and reused in other devices, extending their life and reducing overall waste.

Think about Recycling

Environmental and health reasons

  • Prevents toxic waste:
    Computers contain hazardous materials like lead, mercury, and cadmium. When sent to a landfill, these toxins can seep into the soil and groundwater, polluting ecosystems and posing a risk to human health.

  • Reduces pollution:
    Recycling prevents these harmful chemicals from being released into the air or water, ensuring the environment is not damaged by improper disposal.

Resource conservation

  • Recovers valuable materials:
    Electronics contain valuable, non-renewable resources such as gold, silver, copper, and platinum. Recycling allows these materials to be extracted and reused, reducing the need to mine for new ones.

  • Conserves energy:
    Reusing materials through recycling uses less energy than creating new products from raw materials.

Economic and social reasons

  • Creates jobs:
    The recycling process, including collection, processing, and repurposing, creates jobs and supports the local economy.

  • Supports reuse programs:
    Computers that are still in working condition can be donated or refurbished for use by charities, schools, or community centers, which helps those who may not be able to afford new equipment.

Environmental factors

    • Proper recycling lets us:

  • Keep poisons out of landfills.

    Recover valuable materials (like gold and copper) to use in new products, saving energy and reducing the need for mining.

    • Security

    • Your computer's hard drive stores everything—passwords, bank details, and personal files. Simply hitting delete doesn't erase it permanently.
